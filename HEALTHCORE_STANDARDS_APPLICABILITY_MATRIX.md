# OPES HealthCore — Standards Applicability Matrix

> **Honesty & Compliance Notice**: OPES HealthCore provides *technical software capabilities* aligned with the standards below. Software alone does **not** confer ISO accreditation, GMP compliance, GCP certification, or regulatory approval. Operating organizations remain solely responsible for institutional QMS, SOPs, computer-system validation (IQ/OQ/PQ), clinical safety governance, and accredited third-party assessment.

| Standard / Reference | Applicable Packs / Starters | Technical Capabilities Implemented in SDK | Organizational / External Requirements (Not Provided by SDK) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ISO 15189** (Medical Laboratories — Quality & Competence) | Pack 02 (`diagnostics-core`, `lis-diagnostics`), Pack 07 | Pre-analytical specimen rejection/recollection, TAT SLA breach tracking, quality incident & CAPA logging, pluggable `DiagnosticsQcPolicySpi` | Accredited external proficiency testing (EQA), physical calibration, institutional QMS accreditation |
| **WHO GMP / ICH Q7–Q10** | Pack 03 (`pharma-manufacturing`, `pharma-qms`, `pharma-serialization`) | Versioned Master Formulas, eBMR line clearance, dual-witness weighing, IPC, yield reconciliation, OOS hold blocking CoA, CAPA effectiveness gate, batch genealogy, cold-chain excursion holds | Facility qualification, GMP inspections, Qualified Person (QP) legal sign-off, physical plant SOPs |
| **EU GMP Annex 11 / FDA 21 CFR Part 11** (Technical Controls) | Packs 03, 04 (`persistence-core` `ElectronicSignatureSpi`, `security-audit`) | Maker-checker segregation of duties, meaning-of-signature binding, SHA-256 payload/signature digests, immutable `@ClinicalAudit` trail, optimistic locking | Customer CSV/CSA validation (IQ/OQ/PQ), enterprise PKI/HSM/IdP deployment, periodic audit reviews |
| **GS1 Healthcare / DataMatrix / SSCC** | Pack 01 (`pharmacy-commerce`), Pack 03 (`pharma-serialization`) | `TraceableLotPrimitive` GTIN-14 validation, GS1 AI `(01)/(17)/(10)/(21)` rendering, 18-digit SSCC aggregation, batch recall stop | GS1 company prefix licensing, physical barcode printing/vision systems, national EPCIS hub registration |
| **ISO 20387** (General Requirements for Biobanking) | Pack 04 (`biobanking`) | Pseudonymous donor consent intake & cascading withdrawal, freezer/rack/box/slot mapping, freeze-thaw tracking, aliquot genealogy, cryogenic excursion quarantine, MTA ethics gate & disposal | Institutional biobank governance, ethics committee oversight, physical freezer monitoring calibration |
| **ICH E6(R3) GCP & CDISC (CDASH/SDTM/ADaM)** | Pack 04 (`clinical-research`) | Pseudonymous subject enrollment (`USUBJID`), eligibility + eConsent gate, SDTM-tagged eCRFs, query resolution & PI lock, SAE expedited flagging, de-identified SDTM `DM` JSON export | Sponsor/CRO GCP monitoring, IRB/EC protocol approvals, regulatory eTMF & pharmacovigilance filing |
| **HL7 FHIR R4, IPS, HL7 v2.5.1, IHE PIX/PDQ/XDS.b, DICOMweb** | Packs 02, 05, 06, 07, 08 (`hie-interop`, `fhir-hie`, `hl7-astm-dicom`, `interoperability-ihe`, `radiology`) | HAPI FHIR R4 resources & IPS bundles, HAPI HL7 v2 parsing, ASTM/DICOMweb metadata, IHE XDS.b/PIX/PDQ workflows | National HIE trust-framework onboarding, IHE Connectathon certification, partner mTLS certificates |
