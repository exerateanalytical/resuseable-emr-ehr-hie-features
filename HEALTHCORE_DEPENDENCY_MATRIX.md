## Key Architectural Design Distinctions
1. **Shared Primitives, Never Shared Mutable Domain Entities**: `TraceableLotPrimitive` and `ElectronicSignatureSpi` live in `healthcore-persistence-core` as domain-neutral contracts. Retail Pharmacy (`hc_phcom_*`), Diagnostic Centers (`hc_diag_*`), Biobanking (`hc_biobank_*`), and Pharmaceutical Manufacturing (`hc_pmfg_*`, `hc_pqms_*`, `hc_pser_*`) each maintain dedicated tables, statuses, and approval workflows.
2. **Modality Independence in Pack 02**: `examples/diagnostic-laboratory` installs `diagnostics-core` + `lis-diagnostics` (zero radiology); `examples/radiology-center` installs `diagnostics-core` + `radiology` (zero LIS).
3. **Database Schema Isolation**: Every capability pack ships a dedicated `classpath:db/changelog/packs/pack-*-master.xml` Liquibase master changelog so consumers only materialize tables belonging to their industry.
